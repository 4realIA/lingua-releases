# Lingua privacy policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Lingua is a language-learning app for Russian, Korean, German, Italian and
Hungarian, for Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad. It is made by an individual
developer (Andrew Anton on the App Store, "dmd-dev" on the Microsoft Store).

## What Lingua collects

Nothing. Lingua has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking. It collects no personal information and shares nothing with anyone.

## What stays on your device

Your lesson progress, streak, daily goal and settings are saved on your own
computer, iPhone or iPad so Lingua remembers where you left off. They are never
sent anywhere. Deleting or uninstalling Lingua removes them. On iPhone and
iPad, they are included in your device's own backups (iCloud or computer), as
for any app.

Lingua reads words aloud using the speech voices built into your device
(macOS, Windows, iOS or iPadOS). The text is spoken on your device and isn't
sent to any online service.

## Network use

- **App Store version (iPhone and iPad):** Lingua makes no network
  connections. The App Store handles installing and updating it.
- **Microsoft Store version:** Lingua makes no network connections. The
  Microsoft Store handles installing and updating it.
- **Version downloaded from GitHub (Mac and Windows):** when it opens, and
  every few hours while it's open, Lingua checks a public file on GitHub to
  see whether a new version exists. That request includes no personal
  information. GitHub, like any website, sees the request's IP address; see
  GitHub's privacy statement.

## Children

Lingua is suitable for students of any age and collects no information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.

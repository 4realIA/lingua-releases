# Lingua privacy policy

_Last updated: October 9, 2026_

Lingua is a language-learning app for Russian and Korean. It is made by an
individual developer (publisher "dmd-dev") for family and friends.

## What Lingua collects

Nothing. Lingua has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking.

## What stays on your computer

Your lesson progress, streak, daily goal and settings are saved in a file on
your own computer so Lingua remembers where you left off. They are never sent
anywhere. Uninstalling Lingua removes them.

Lingua reads words aloud using the speech voices built into Windows or macOS.
The text is spoken on your computer and isn't sent to any online service.

## Network use

- **Microsoft Store version:** Lingua makes no network connections. The
  Microsoft Store handles installing and updating it.
- **Version downloaded from GitHub:** when it opens, and every few hours while
  it's open, Lingua checks a public file on GitHub to see whether a new version
  exists. That request includes no personal information. GitHub, like any
  website, sees the request's IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.

## Children

Lingua is suitable for students and collects no information from anyone,
including children.
